How we keep a record

Care in the field.
Context in the record.

A useful observation keeps enough of its circumstances to be understood by someone returning to it later.

01 / CONTEXT

Keep the circumstances

Time, place, method and conditions travel with a record. Device settings and field notes help distinguish a change in the environment from a change in how it was observed.

02 / ORIGINALS

Leave a way back

Original images and readings remain separate from summaries. Consistent names, simple manifests and checksums make materials easier to organise, exchange and revisit.

03 / REVIEW

Make uncertainty visible

Missing intervals, doubtful identifications and unusual readings are kept in view. Periodic review helps relate contributions without smoothing away the limits of the source material.

04 / CONTINUITY

Work in series

Related records are grouped by place, period and observation method. Tables, image sequences and shared notes support comparison across visits, devices and contributors.

The aim is a record that remains useful beyond the moment it was made.
