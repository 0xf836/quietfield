What we pay attention to

Different records.
Shared context.

Some observations are made in a moment. Others take shape through repeated visits or a device left quietly in place.

01 / CONDITIONS

Weather & conditions

Temperature, rainfall, humidity and the conditions around a visit. Instrument readings and notes from the field offer complementary ways to follow a place through changing weather.

02 / WILDLIFE

Wildlife & seasonal patterns

Bird sightings, animal traces and familiar routes. Repeated observations help place a brief encounter within the quieter patterns of movement, return and seasonal change.

03 / REMOTE

Remote observations

Camera traps and small field sensors extend attention between visits. Images and readings are considered alongside placement notes, recording intervals and the limits of each device.
