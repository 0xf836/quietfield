Following the ordinary

A temperature reading, a bird returning to the same branch, a camera left to watch a quiet crossing. Each offers a partial view. Kept with its context, it becomes something we can return to.

Digital Field Station brings these different kinds of observation into a common practice. Contributions take different forms and arrive at different intervals; the connection is a careful record of what was noticed, where and when.
